# Phishing Awareness Training

## 📌 Overview
This project is a training presentation created as part of the CodeAlpha 
Cybersecurity Internship. It is designed to educate employees/users on how 
to recognize, avoid, and respond to phishing attacks — one of the most 
common and effective cyberattack methods.

## 🎯 Objective
- Understand common phishing techniques
- Identify phishing emails and suspicious links
- Recognize social engineering tactics
- Learn best practices for preventing phishing attacks

## 📂 Contents
- [Presentation](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ZLnFdbtLyKxEfojtSls1kha6DhqimEuS?usp=sharing)

## 📝 Topics Covered
- What is phishing?
- Types of phishing attacks (email phishing, spear phishing, smishing, 
  vishing, etc.)
- Common phishing indicators 
- Real-world examples/case studies
- Best practices to avoid falling victim
- What to do if you suspect a phishing attempt

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Microsoft PowerPoint

## 🎓 Key Takeaways
By the end of this training, users/staff should be able to:
- Confidently identify common phishing tactics
- Avoid clicking on suspicious links or downloading unverified attachments
- Report phishing attempts through proper channels

## ✅ Conclusion
This training highlights how simple awareness and vigilance can 
significantly reduce the risk of falling victim to phishing attacks. By 
understanding common tactics and warning signs, people are better equipped 
to protect themselves and their organizations from this widespread 
cybersecurity threat.
